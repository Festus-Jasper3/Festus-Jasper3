Benefits, Limitations and Ethical Issues

Benefits

  • 24/7 availability
  • Fast response time
  • Cost efficiency

Limitations

  • Limited emotional understanding
  • Difficulty handling complex queries

Ethical Issues

Ethical concerns related to chatbots include data privacy, bias in AI systems, and over-reliance on automated decision-making. Responsible use of chatbot technology is essential.
