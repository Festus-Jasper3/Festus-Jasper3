Natural Language Processing (NLP)

Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a branch of artificial intelligence that enables computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language. NLP is a core component of modern chatbot systems.

Key NLP Processes

  • Tokenization
  • Part-of-Speech Tagging
  • Intent Detection
  • Entity Recognition

Challenges in NLP

Human language is complex and often ambiguous. Chatbots may struggle with understanding context, slang, sarcasm, and multiple languages, which makes NLP a challenging area of AI research.
