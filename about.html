Introduction to Chatbots

A chatbot is a software application that enables interaction between humans and computers using natural language. This interaction may occur through text-based messaging platforms or voice-based interfaces. Chatbots are designed to understand user input and respond in a manner that resembles human conversation.

The development of chatbots began in the 1960s with early programs such as ELIZA, which used pattern matching techniques to simulate conversation. Although early chatbots were limited in intelligence, they laid the foundation for modern conversational systems. With advancements in computing power, data availability, and artificial intelligence techniques, chatbots have become more intelligent and capable. Modern chatbots often incorporate machine learning and NLP to understand user intent, manage context, and provide more accurate responses.

Types of Chatbots

Rule-Based ChatbotsThese chatbots operate using predefined rules and decision trees. They respond to user inputs based on specific keywords or commands. While simple and reliable, they lack flexibility and cannot handle complex or unexpected queries.

AI-Based Chatbots These chatbots use artificial intelligence techniques such as machine learning and natural language processing. These chatbots can understand user intent, learn from interactions, and improve performance over time.
