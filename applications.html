Applications of Chatbots

Chatbots are used across many industries to improve efficiency and user experience.

  • Customer Service: Handling frequently asked questions.
  • Education: Supporting learning and academic guidance.
  • Healthcare: Appointment scheduling and health information.
  • Banking: Account inquiries and customer support.
  • Churches: Sharing sermons and event information.
