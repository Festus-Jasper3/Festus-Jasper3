Welcome to Chatbot Technology

Chatbots are intelligent computer programs designed to simulate human conversation through text or voice-based interactions. With rapid advancements in Artificial Intelligence (AI), chatbots have evolved from simple rule-based systems into sophisticated tools capable of understanding natural language, learning from user interactions, and providing meaningful responses in real time.

In today’s digital world, chatbots are widely used across various sectors including business, education, healthcare, banking, and religious organizations. They help automate repetitive tasks, improve user experience, and provide instant access to information.

This website explores the fundamental concepts behind chatbot technology, focusing on how chatbots work, the role of Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing (NLP), and their applications in real-world environments.
